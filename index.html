ARCHIVE LETTER · OCT 2026

To My Forever Muse

AIRMAIL

Eighteen feels like a big number, but when I look at you, I see every chapter all at once: the little girl holding a flower with the brightest eyes, the visionary artist lost in her sketchbook, and the radiant young woman standing right on the edge of the world.

You carry the bold pulse of New York, the effortless charm of Paris, and the quiet poetry of London—yet the warmest place you inhabit will always be right beside me.

No distance, train tracks, or busy schedule will ever change the telepathy we share. Step into this year knowing you are fiercely loved, protected, and destined for art that outlives time.

Endlessly in your corner,
Your Sister ♡
